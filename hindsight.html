Hindsight bias

Once the ending is known, that path feels like the only one.

Walk the maze. Then look back at the path.

Before the ending. At each turn in the maze, more than one path is still open. You do not yet know which one comes out.

After the ending. Once you are out, the path you took feels marked, and the maze itself did not change.
