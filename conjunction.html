Conjunction fallacy

Extra details can make a story feel more likely than it is.

Which is more likely: the short story, or the short story plus an extra detail?

Anyone who is a teller and in the club is already a teller.

The longer description picks out a piece of the shorter one. It does not pick out a bigger piece.

The longer story was never ahead. When every teller is in the club, the two counts match. Otherwise the teller row is the bigger one.
