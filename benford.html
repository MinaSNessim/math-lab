Benford's law

In real lists, 1 is a first digit more often than 9.

Look at the first digits. Which digit shows up most?

The coral figure is the first digit. A list that spreads across many sizes is not the same as counting 1, 2, 3.

The first digit is the leftmost figure. 148 and 19 both start with 1.

Numbers that spread across many sizes put 1 first more often than 9. A plain count does not.

In the growing list, 1 leads about 30% of the time and 9 leads about 5%. Counting in order, each digit from 1 to 9 leads about 11% of the time.
