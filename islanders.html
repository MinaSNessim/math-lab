Blue-eyed islanders

They leave on the nth night, and only because they all heard the guru.

Everyone can see the others’ eye colors but not their own. A guru says: “I see someone with yellow eyes.” If you deduce your own eye color, you leave that night.

Night —

The guru’s public statement creates common knowledge.

Each night that no one leaves gives everyone new information.

On the night that matches the real number of yellow-eyed people, they all deduce their eye color and leave.

Muddy children are the same count. Aumann's agreement theorem uses the same public step.
