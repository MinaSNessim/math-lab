Colored 32. Empty 0. The long edge bends in.

Colored squares stay 32. The empty count is the square on the picture, 0 or 1.

The long edge bends in when the empty count is 0, and out when it is 1. The dashed line is the straight edge.

A straight edge would close 32.5 squares. The bend is the empty square. The four pieces did not change size.
