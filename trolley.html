Trolley problem

You can send the harm the other way. Both ways have a cost.

A trolley is heading toward five people. You can pull a lever to send it toward one person instead. What do you do?

Hit —

Pulling the lever saves five and kills one.

Doing nothing lets five die.

Many people accept the lever but reject pushing someone directly for the same outcome.
