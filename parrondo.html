Parrondo's paradox

Each game loses. Switching between them can win.

Each machine, played alone, drifts down. What happens if you switch between them?

Machine A wins 49.5% of plays. Left on its own, the coins drift down.

Machine B wins 9.5% of plays when your coins are a multiple of 3, and 74.5% otherwise. Left on its own, it drifts down too.

Switching between the machines drifts up. Each machine, played by itself, drifts down.
