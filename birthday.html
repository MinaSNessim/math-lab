Birthday paradox

How full before two people share a day?

How full does the room get before two people share a day?

Fill a room and look for a shared day. The day number sits on each matching pair.

Lock a share, then run the rooms.
—This room
—Rooms with a shared day
—Long-run share for this size

Each birthday is one of 365 days, spread evenly. Leap day is left out.

A shared day is any two people in the room, not a match with you alone.

With 23 people there are 253 pairs. A shared birthday shows up in about half of rooms.
